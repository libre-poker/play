The room

Heads-up fixed-limit hold'em against a near-unexploitable trainer: solved preflop (Cepheus), trained middle streets, every river solved exactly as you play it. Blinds 10/20, stacks reset each hand.

Keys — 1 fold · 2 check/call · 3 bet/raise · 4 coach · 5 leak meter.
